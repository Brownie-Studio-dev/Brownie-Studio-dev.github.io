FicBurrow 隐私政策

Effective date: November 20, 2026

FicBurrow 是在浏览器中运行的阅读书库工具。本政策说明它会使用哪些信息，以及这些信息会发送到哪里。

保存在设备上的信息

收藏链接、标题、评分、备注、文件夹、阅读状态和进度保存在浏览器或用户脚本管理器的本地存储中。网盘连接设置和认证信息也保存在本机，用于连接你选择的服务。

可选同步服务

如果你连接 Dropbox 或坚果云、Nextcloud 等 WebDAV 服务，FicBurrow 会将跨设备同步所需的书库数据和阅读活动发送到你选择的服务与账户。服务商按照其自身条款和隐私政策处理这些信息。认证信息用于连接你选择的服务，不包含在同步书库中。

网页与可选服务

FicBurrow 会读取你访问的受支持网站页面，以识别作品、导入你选择的条目或检查更新。账户导入使用浏览器现有登录状态，不会替你点赞、关注或修改这些网站账户。

如果你使用简介翻译或存档查询功能，相关公开文本或作品网址会发送给你选择的翻译或存档服务。该服务按照自身政策处理请求。

发送给开发者的信息

FicBurrow 不包含开发者分析、广告追踪或开发者遥测。你的书库和网盘认证信息不会发送给开发者。

你的选择

同步、翻译和存档查询均为可选功能。你可以断开网盘、关闭可选功能，并从应用中导出书库。部分界面偏好仅保存在当前设备。

联系

隐私问题请联系 browniestudio.dev@gmail.com。

FicBurrow Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

FicBurrow is a reading library that runs in your browser. This policy explains what information it uses and where that information goes.

Information stored on your device

Your saved links, titles, ratings, notes, folders, reading status, and reading progress are stored locally by your browser or userscript manager. Connection settings and credentials for a sync provider are also kept locally so FicBurrow can connect to the service you choose.

Optional sync services

If you connect Dropbox or a WebDAV service such as Nutstore or Nextcloud, FicBurrow sends the library data and reading activity needed to sync to the provider and account you select. Those services process the data under their own terms and privacy policies. Credentials are used to authenticate with your selected provider and are not included in the synced library.

Web pages and optional services

FicBurrow reads pages you visit on supported sites to identify works, import items you request, or check for updates. Account imports use your existing browser session and do not like, follow, or otherwise change your account on those sites.

If you choose a translation or archive lookup feature, the relevant public text or work URL is sent to the translation or archive service you selected. That service handles the request under its own policy.

Information sent to the developer

FicBurrow does not include developer analytics, advertising trackers, or developer telemetry. Your library and provider credentials are not sent to the developer.

Your choices

Sync and translation or archive lookups are optional. You can disconnect a sync provider, disable optional features, and export your library from the app. Some display preferences are stored only on the current device.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact browniestudio.dev@gmail.com.
